How to use this test
  1. Use wired headphones first. Start audio, then permit autoplay if your browser asks.
  2. Face the Mac camera and click Set forward while facing forward. The source is initially placed at your displayed right-front and then fixed to the Mac camera; use Set source from head to reset that initial placement. Mirror display + audio mirrors the webcam preview and HRTF. World is a room view from behind you looking at the Mac, so your left/right matches the scene.
  3. Enable threshold clicks, return below the threshold, and turn right past it. Export the head-audio trial table when done.
  4. Hold the iPhone as the saber. Sit back until the camera sees your shoulders and the holding hand (cyan skeleton + wrist dot). In ZIG SIM enable GYRO, QUATERNION, and GRAVITY. Restart ./run.zsh. Lay the phone flat, top toward the Mac, then click Set saber reference (this also lets acceleration map into the room). The handle follows the wrist at camera rate and IMU-coasts between frames or when the hand leaves the picture; the blade follows IMU orientation.
  5. The Uno/MPU6050 path is optional legacy: ./run.zsh --serial /dev/cu.usbmodem….
  6. Repeat with Bluetooth headphones without changing any software settings; compare both the browser estimate and the phone-video measurement.
